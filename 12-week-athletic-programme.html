TRAINING PILLARS
Four non-negotiable disciplines. Each one builds a different adaptation. Together they create the functional athlete profile — powerful, fast, mobile, and durable at 35 and beyond.
🏃
Zone 2 Running
Your aerobic engine. 80% of all running.
  • HR 135–150 bpm — conversational
  • 6:00–7:00 min/km pace (feels slow — it is)
  • Nasal breathing as a quality check
  • Long run: 80–115 min Saturday
  • VO₂ max target: 52+ by week 12
⚡
Speed & Power
Your missing piece. Builds fast-twitch capacity.
  • VO₂ max intervals: 4–6×4 min @95% HR
  • Hill sprints: 8–10×8s max effort
  • 40m accelerations: 6–10 reps full recovery
  • Med ball throws: rotational power
  • Box jumps: loaded & unloaded
🏋️
Compound Strength
Structural load. 3× per week. Progressive overload.
  • Back squat → target 1.5× BW (114 kg)
  • Deadlift → target 2× BW (152 kg)
  • Overhead press → target 0.8× BW (61 kg)
  • Trap bar carry: loaded locomotion
  • Power clean: full-body explosiveness
🤸
Calisthenics
Body control. Relative strength. Athletic identity.
  • Weighted pull-up → +28 kg target
  • Muscle-up: 3–5 reps by week 12
  • HSPU: 6–8 strict reps
  • L-sit: 30 sec hold
  • Front lever: 10 sec tuck progression
🧘
Mobility & Sleep
Where adaptation actually happens. Daily investment.
  • 10 min morning mobility — non-negotiable
  • Bed by 11:00–11:15 PM every night
  • Weekly yoga session 45–60 min
  • Hip flexor + thoracic + ankle daily
  • Body Battery 75+ = green light to train hard

Fenix 8 integration
SYNC TO YOUR WATCH
Three ways to get this programme onto your Garmin Fenix 8 — from fully automatic to manual. The best option for your needs is TrainingPeaks: it supports auto-sync of structured running and strength-adjacent workouts with HR targets direct to your watch.
01
TrainingPeaks → Garmin Auto-Sync
Effort: 30 min setup · then fully automatic
TrainingPeaks has native two-way integration with Garmin Connect. Once connected, the next 15 days of structured workouts auto-sync to your watch daily. Your Fenix 8 will show each session with HR targets, interval structure, and step-by-step guidance on the wrist.
  • 1
    Create a free TrainingPeaks account at trainingpeaks.com
  • 2
    Go to Settings → Apps & Devices → Add Garmin → log in with your Garmin Connect credentials
  • 3
    Enable "Structured Workout Sync" toggle — this pushes workouts TO your watch
  • 4
    Add this programme's sessions to your TrainingPeaks calendar (or ask a coach to load them)
  • 5
    Sync your Fenix 8 via Bluetooth — workouts appear under Training → Training Calendar
  • 6
    On race/workout day: start activity → Options → Training → Today's Workout → Go
02
Garmin Connect — Built-in Workout Creator
Effort: 2–3 hrs setup · semi-automatic
Build each session directly in Garmin Connect's workout creator (web or app). Define steps, HR zones, duration, and rest periods. Schedule them on your calendar. They auto-push to your Fenix 8 on the day. No third-party app needed — works natively.
  • 1
    Open Garmin Connect app → More → Workouts → Create Workout
  • 2
    Select sport type (Running, Strength, etc.) and add steps with HR targets
  • 3
    Save workout → Schedule it on the Calendar for the correct date
  • 4
    Repeat for all 12 weeks (this is the time-consuming part)
  • 5
    Sync watch — workouts appear automatically on the scheduled day
  • 6
    Note: Strength workouts have limited structured support vs running workouts
03
Final Surge → Garmin Auto-Sync
Effort: 20 min setup · 4-day rolling auto-sync
Final Surge is a free coaching platform with direct Garmin integration. It auto-pushes the next 4 days of structured workouts to your Garmin Connect calendar each night, which then sync to your Fenix 8. Good alternative if you want coaching features.
  • 1
    Download Final Surge app → create free account
  • 2
    More → Connected Apps → Garmin Connect → Sync Accounts
  • 3
    Enable Auto-Sync toggle — next 4 days push nightly automatically
  • 4
    Add workouts to your Final Surge calendar
  • 5
    Or tap Manual Workout Push to send immediately
04
On-Watch Manual — Simplest Option
Effort: None · No sync needed
Your Fenix 8 has built-in workout suggestions and the ability to set HR alerts and interval timers directly on the watch. Use this as a fallback — set HR zone alerts (zone 2 alert when you go above 145 bpm) and use the interval timer for sprint and strength sessions.
  • 1
    Fenix 8 → Settings → Alerts → Heart Rate → set high alert at 145 bpm for easy runs
  • 2
    For intervals: Training → Workouts → HIIT → Tabata or Custom Intervals
  • 3
    For strength: use the Strength activity with rep counting
  • 4
    Screenshot this plan and refer to it before each session
Recommendation for you: Start with Option 4 (on-watch HR alerts) this week during recovery — zero setup time and immediately fixes your biggest issue (easy runs too hard). Then set up TrainingPeaks (Option 1) over the weekend and load the full 12-week calendar before Week 1 begins. The 30-minute setup pays dividends for 12 weeks of guided sessions appearing automatically on your wrist each morning.

Key watch setting to change today: Fenix 8 → Run activity → Alerts → Add HR Alert → Above 145 bpm. This single setting will beep every time your "easy" runs drift into Zone 4 — the most important change from your current training.
